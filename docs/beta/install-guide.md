# Aedrova beta: install guide

This is the private beta. It runs on Mac (macOS 14 or newer, Apple silicon). Windows is not
included yet.

## Before you start

- You need the beta password from your invite. It opens the download on aedrova.com.
- Sign in with the email your invite was sent to. Only invited emails can use the beta.
  - Google sign-in works for Gmail and Google Workspace addresses.
  - For any other address (for example Yahoo), use email and password sign-in.
  - Do not use Apple's "Hide My Email". Aedrova cannot match it to your invite.

## Install

1. Go to aedrova.com, open **Plans**, and find **Part of the Beta?** below the plans.
2. Enter the beta password and choose **Continue**. You land on the download page.
3. Download **Aedrova.dmg**, open it, and drag **Aedrova** into **Applications**.
4. Open Aedrova from Applications. The first launch can take a few seconds.

## First launch

1. A short, full-screen introduction opens. Press **Escape** to leave full screen, not to cancel.
2. **Sign in.** You return to the app when it finishes.
3. **Allow what you need.** Microphone, Camera and Screen each have an **Allow** button. macOS
   shows its own prompt next. Nothing is required, and you can change these later in System
   Settings, Privacy and Security.
4. **Create or join a workspace.** To join a teammate's workspace, use the join code they share
   with **Share join code** in the sidebar.
5. **Connect a project folder** if you want the agent to build for you (optional).
6. A tour of the workspace starts. It can be skipped.

On later launches Aedrova reopens your workspace without the introduction.

## What to try

- **Chat and agent:** write `@Aedrova` and ask a question, or ask it to build something. Builds
  happen in a safe copy of your project. Nothing changes in your folder until you choose
  **Review changes** and apply them.
- **Meeting notes:** during a Zoom, Teams or Google Meet call, a small pill offers **Take
  meeting notes**. Audio is transcribed on your Mac, and only text is shared when you press
  **Confirm**. The first time downloads a 148 MB speech model.
- **Buds:** add a Bud, give it a role, and connect GitHub, Notion, Figma or Supabase.
- **Slack:** import a Slack export from the introduction or from workspace settings.
- **Browser:** the Browser button opens a panel the agent can use.

## If something goes wrong

Send a note to the feedback address in your invite. Include what you did, what you expected,
and what happened. Include your macOS version and the Aedrova version (Aedrova menu, About).
Please do not send message contents or exports.
