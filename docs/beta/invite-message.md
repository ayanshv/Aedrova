**Get the app**
1. Open aedrova-beta-staging.onrender.com/plans and find "Part of the Beta?" under the plans.
2. Enter this password: {password}
3. Download Aedrova for your computer and sign in with this email address ({email}).

**On Mac** (macOS 14 or newer, Apple silicon): open the download, drag Aedrova into
Applications, then open it from there.

**On Windows**: unzip the download and open Aedrova.exe inside the Aedrova folder. This
beta build isn't signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC." Choose
**More info**, then **Run anyway**. It's safe, and signing is coming.

The site is on a free server and can take up to a minute to wake the first time. If the page
is slow to load, wait a moment and refresh.
