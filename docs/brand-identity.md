# Aedrova — brand identity direction

October 4, 2026. Proposed identity for owner review. This defines a direction; it does
not change shipped UI, public website copy, availability claims or feature gates.

## The idea

**Shared understanding becomes working software.**

Aedrova brings a team's conversations, approved decisions and authorized context into
the place where it builds. The team remains the author of the direction. The agent helps
carry that direction through. The emotional promise is continuity: less repeating,
less losing the thread, more confidence that the work reflects what the team meant.

Brand personality: thoughtful, precise, capable and quietly ambitious. Warmth comes from
clear language and helpful behavior, not exaggerated friendliness. Premium means care,
reliability and clarity, not expensive-looking decoration.

Audience: teams that want to build together and keep their product understanding intact.
Avoid defining the product as a casual group chat or merely a collection of integrations.

## Message system

- Primary brand line: **From a thought to a thing.** Preserve the existing line.
- Differentiation line: **Build on what your team knows.**
- Product descriptor: **The workspace where teams and AI build together.**
- Behavior line: **Quiet until you need it.** Preserve the existing principle.
- Supporting explanation: Bring the conversation, the decision and the build into one
  workspace, with the context intact.

Use one principal line per composition. Do not stack every slogan on the same page.
Brand lines describe the direction; supporting feature claims must match actual availability.
Do not promise complete product memory or meeting understanding before M14–M17 acceptance.

## Visual identity

Keep the supplied glass ribbon mark. Its interlocking forms can represent people,
context and work coming together; this is a proposed brand interpretation, not an
assertion about the artist's original intent. The negative space is as important as
the surface. Do not redraw it as a generic AI sparkle, add busy halos or rotate it forever.

Use the wordmark **Aedrova**, consistently capitalized, in the system font with medium
or semibold weight and restrained tracking. The team may name its agent; the product,
website, app icon and organization-facing identity remain Aedrova.

For logo use, keep clear space of at least one quarter of the visible mark's width.
Measure the visible artwork, not the export canvas; trim excess transparent margins
when producing dedicated icon assets. Use the symbol at 24 px or larger only after
checking legibility at actual size. A future small-size vector variant needs its own
approved asset; do not imply one exists. Never stretch, recolor or put the logo in every row.

Palette grounded in the current desktop theme:

- Light: `#F5F5F7` background, `#FFFFFF` surface, `#1D1D1F` primary text.
- Dark: `#000000` background, `#1C1C1E` surface, `#F5F5F7` primary text.
- Action blue: `#0066CC` light, `#0A84FF` dark.
- The supplied logo's blue, lilac and mint remain artwork accents. Mint is not a
  broad interface theme. Status colors retain their semantic meaning.

Use the owner's 60–30–10 rule as approximate visual emphasis: 60% base, 30% supporting
neutral surfaces, no more than 10% color emphasis. It is not a requirement to fill 10%
with blue. Avoid pastel body text, giant colored panels and gradients on every component.

System fonts only. Clear display headings, readable body copy and quiet metadata;
medium/semibold emphasis instead of repeated bold. A 4 px spacing scale, generous
section spacing, 20–24 px main container radii and restrained smaller control radii.
Glass belongs primarily in navigation and overlays; long-form content needs stable
contrast. Focus rings, error text and labels must remain readable in both themes.

## Signature behavior

The memorable brand element should be the way understanding persists, not another effect.
Make **a traceable thread** the product signature: a confirmed decision can lead to a
build and reviewed result without losing its source. Implement this through M15–M17
using actual records and citations, not a decorative claim of understanding.

The agent participates as a named teammate in the conversation. Its temporary work
messages give way to a concise result, with evidence available on demand. It does not
interrupt ordinary conversation, imply work when idle, or publish without permission.
Conflicts become understandable questions, failures become clear next actions, and
consent is visible before capture or reuse.

Motion communicates a state change. Target brief 160–240 ms control transitions and
300–450 ms view changes; use subtle settling rather than large bounces. A restrained
left-to-right light pass can denote real agent activity. No perpetual logo motion,
confetti on ordinary actions or ornamental loading. Honor reduced motion. Sounds are
optional, short and useful; no sound is required to understand state.

## Voice

Lead with what happened, then the next useful action. Short sentences, concrete nouns,
ordinary words. Be confident about verified facts and candid about limits.

- Working: “Reviewing the team's decisions…”
- Clarification: “These two decisions conflict. Which should I follow?”
- Result: “Ready to review. I updated three files and ran the checks.” Only if verified.
- Failure: “The build stopped because the provider limit was reached. Your project is unchanged.”
  Use the last sentence only if no project changes occurred.
- Permission: “Push these changes to GitHub?” Show the actual diff and destination.
- Meeting consent: “Allow your audio to be transcribed for this meeting?”
- Waitlist: “Join the waitlist.” Confirmation: “You're on the list.” Only after storage succeeds.

Avoid “revolutionary,” “magic,” “supercharge,” “10x,” “knows everything,” “100% secure,”
false certainty, fabricated test results and “Oops!” for serious failures. Do not obscure
agent limits or call captured screen/video automatic visual understanding.

## Photography and demonstration

Actual product captures are the proof. Show readable light and dark screens, real
interactions and current features. No fake dashboards or staged claims of working calls.
The website's city footage can introduce human connection, but stays subordinate to the
interface and headline. Do not let it become Aedrova's only recognizable brand element.

Demonstrations should emphasize an accessible source, an agreed decision, the build
and the reviewable result once each is implemented. Keep copy minimal; the work supplies
the evidence. Avoid fictional success stories presented as real customer outcomes.

## Rollout

This proposal requires owner direction before changing the live identity. Audit existing
copy and components against it during approved implementation milestones. Each newly
accepted differentiation feature gets real website captures and accurate availability
copy in that milestone, not in advance. Keep the public waitlist while release gates remain.

Maintain one recognisable mark, one type system, restrained neutral/blue palettes and
consistent behavior across app, website, checkout, installer, email and documentation.
Apple is a reference for care and restraint; Aedrova's identity is shared understanding
and continuity of team work. Do not reuse Apple's slogans, marks or pretend affiliation.
