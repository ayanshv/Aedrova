# UI audit — November 22, 2026

Goal: an official, minimal, Apple-like feel. Method: offscreen captures of the main window,
Work/Files/People tabs, workspace settings (all 11 sections), personal settings, Bud setup and
the build dialog in light and dark, plus a scan of the source for token drift. Nothing was
changed by this audit.

## Verdict

The structure is sound and the brand mark is good, but the app reads as a well-made internal
tool rather than a finished product. The causes are not one big flaw. They are many small
inconsistencies, leftover "preview" language, settings pages that are mostly empty, and a
design system that is not enforced. Apple's feel comes from restraint and consistency, so the
fix is mostly subtraction and unification.

## P0: things that make it feel unfinished

1. **"Preview" and "alpha" language is everywhere.** The window title says "Local preview"
   (`window.py:62`, `:775`), there is a "Local preview" badge, a Help item "About this
   preview", "Internal preview" and "internal alpha" copy in settings (`preferences.py:178`,
   `:187`), and connector states like "Setup required", "Coming next", "Not checked".
   A shipped product never labels itself. Hide or reword all of it.
2. **Raw timestamps.** Messages show `2026-09-27 05:00`. Use "5:00 AM", "Yesterday", "Mon"
   with the full date on hover. This is the single fastest premium upgrade.
3. **Settings pages that are a single button.** Buds shows a title and a full-width blue
   "Open buds". Billing shows "Open billing & credits". Each is a dead page that redirects.
   Either put the content inline or make these rows in a list with a chevron.
4. **The settings window chrome is wrong.** The logo, a "Connected" caption and the title
   stack at the top-left with no hierarchy. The section list is clipped at the bottom
   ("Knowledge & context" is cut off, with a visible scrollbar). Large areas are empty.
5. **Unlabeled icon row.** The sidebar has four icon buttons (invite, tune, link, people)
   with no labels. People will not know what they do. Label them, or move each into the
   place it belongs.
6. **Dialogs are separate windows.** Bud setup, build, settings and review open as free
   windows. On macOS these should be sheets attached to the main window, or inline panels.
   Separate windows are the strongest "utility app" signal.

## P1: inconsistency that people feel without naming it

7. **Type scale drift.** Font sizes in use: 10, 11, 12, 14, 16, 17, 18, 20, 22, 24, 30 px.
   33 places use 12 px. Define five steps (for example 12 caption, 13 secondary, 15 body,
   20 title, 28 large title) and nothing else. Body at 14 with captions at 11 is too small
   for the dark theme.
8. **Radius drift.** 14 different corner radii (1 to 16 px) are in use. Pick two: 8 for
   controls, 14 for panels and cards, with fully round only for avatars and pills.
9. **Three button shapes.** "Continue" is a full pill, "Start build" is a rounded
   rectangle, "Open buds" is a square-cornered full-width bar. One primary style, one
   secondary, one text button. Primary actions are right-aligned or centered, never
   hard-left (build dialog).
10. **Three input styles.** The search field is outlined, settings fields are gray-filled,
    the composer has a bright border. Choose one (filled, no border, subtle focus ring).
11. **Hard-coded colors in 15 modules** (`#4388F5`, white borders, `#1759bf` and `#8fbcff`
    links and others) bypass the theme, which is how a swatch ring was invisible in the
    light theme. Route everything through theme tokens.
12. **Icons are mixed.** Real outline icons sit beside text glyphs (`›`, `▸`, `▾`, `✓`,
    `●`) and arrows inside labels ("Continue →" 34 times). Weights and baselines differ.
    Use one icon set, and drop arrows from button text.
13. **Duplicate icons for different things.** Overview and Account share the same grid
    icon; Imported history, Channels and Knowledge share the same document icon.
14. **Heavy focus state.** The composer keeps a bright blue 1.5 px outline whenever focused,
    which reads as an error or selection. Use a soft 3 px halo, and only for keyboard
    focus.
15. **Header noise.** "Connected" is shown as text beside the theme toggle. Show connection
    status only when something is wrong. The search field's grey-on-grey placeholder is low
    contrast.
16. **Rows that do not look tappable.** On the Work tab, "Queue & run history" and "Shared
    build evidence" are bordered bars with no icon or chevron, and look like text fields.
17. **Empty states are not designed.** "Your startup's context. Connect your first Bud."
    floats mid-sidebar with no icon or button. The channel starts with a "Shared
    conversation" caption and a huge blank area. Each empty state needs one icon, one
    sentence and one action.

## P2: polish

18. **Dense metadata.** 276 middle dots (`·`) are used as separators in meta text; many lines
    carry three facts. Trim to what matters.
19. **Muted text contrast** in dark mode is below comfortable reading levels for 11 to 12 px
    text. Raise it, or enlarge the text.
20. **Composer hierarchy.** Permission selector, "Ask Aedrova", an overflow menu, mic and a
    disabled Send all have similar weight. Make Send the only filled control, and fold
    secondary controls into the "+" menu.
21. **Avatar styles.** Human avatars are gray squircles with initials; Bud and agent avatars
    are illustrated and round. Pick one shape and treat the illustration as the exception.
22. **Brand repetition.** The full logo and wordmark reappear inside settings. Show it once,
    in the main title bar.
23. **Motion language.** Buttons spring, the new dots animate, but sheets and panels appear
    instantly. Use one short ease-out (160 to 220 ms) for everything that appears or
    resizes, and honor Reduce Motion.
24. **Dialog bottoms.** Footer buttons differ per window ("Workspace settings", "Log out",
    "Done" as three styles). Standardise: one primary on the right, one text button on
    the left.

## What already works

- The brand mark, wordmark and Bud illustrations are distinctive.
- The light and dark themes both exist and keep the same layout.
- The new agent task list, approval and question cards use a consistent card style and are
  a good basis for the system.
- Reduced-motion support exists across the animated pieces.

## Proposed order of work

1. **Pass 1, no layout change (about 1 day):** remove preview language, human timestamps,
   type and radius scales, one button shape, theme tokens for hard-coded colors, composer
   focus ring and hierarchy.
2. **Pass 2, structure (2 to 3 days):** settings as an inset-grouped Apple-style sidebar
   with real inline content, labeled sidebar actions, sheets instead of free windows,
   designed empty states.
3. **Pass 3, finish (1 to 2 days):** one icon set, motion pass, dark-theme contrast, and a
   fresh light/dark capture of every screen as a regression reference.

## Pass 1 status (November 22, 2026)

Done in source, not yet in a signed build:
- "Preview" and "alpha" wording removed. Demo mode is labeled "Demo" and only shows when the
  app is actually in demo mode. Connector states read "Unavailable" and "Checking…".
- Message times are human ("5:00 AM", "Yesterday 5:00 AM", "Mon 5:00 AM", "Sep 27, 5:00 AM")
  in the Mac's own 12 or 24 hour style, for stored and live messages.
- Corner radii reduced to two (8 controls, 12 panels) plus the 16 px popup menus you asked
  for earlier. Every button, including onboarding and Bud setup, uses the 8 px shape.
- Text sizes of 10 and 11 px raised to 12; 17 to 18; 22 to 24. Body stays 14.
- Focus rings are soft (translucent accent) instead of hard outlines, including the composer.
- The "Connected" caption is hidden; only demo mode shows a badge.
- Link colors come from one theme token instead of hard-coded blues.

Still open from this audit: sections 3 to 6 (settings pages, unlabeled icons, sheets), 12
to 13 (one icon set), 16 to 17 (row affordances and empty states), 20 to 24.

## Pass 2 status (November 22, 2026)

Done in source: the sidebar's four unlabeled icons are labeled rows (Invite teammates,
Workspace settings, Connectors, People and status); the empty Buds area explains itself and
has one action; the Work tab rows are an inset group with icons, detail lines and chevrons;
settings pages that only held a blue button are inset rows with live detail (Bud count,
billing capacity); the stray logo and "Connected" caption are gone from settings; Account,
Channels, Imported history and Knowledge no longer share one icon; the usage meter shows the
5-hour window by default and opens a weekly and 5-hour view on click.

Not done: macOS sheets instead of free windows. Sheets change modality and focus, and I can
only verify them on a real Mac window, so I left them for a supervised pass. Pass 3 (one icon
set, motion language, dark contrast) is still open.

## Pass 3 status (November 22, 2026)

Done in source:
- **Icons:** one drawn icon set. Typed arrows and chevrons (`→ ↗ ‹ › ▸ ▾ ⌄`) are gone from
  button labels, the sidebar, inset rows, the pinned banner and the completed-steps toggle;
  a test fails if one comes back. A drawn right chevron was added.
- **Motion:** `motion.py` defines three durations (120, 180, 260 ms) and one ease-out curve,
  all skipped under Reduce Motion. Cards, the usage popover, dialogs and settings pages fade in
  with them; stray timings were aligned to the scale.
- **Contrast:** every text and surface pairing in both themes meets WCAG AA (4.5:1), enforced by
  `tests/test_theme_contrast.py`. Fixes: link and accent text in dark mode (was 2.7 to 3.8:1)
  now uses a lighter link color, and light-theme muted text was darkened.

Still open from this audit: macOS sheets (needs a supervised pass on a real Mac), a
VoiceOver and Narrator pass, and a before and after review on a real display.
